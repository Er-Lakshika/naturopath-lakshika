FAQ

Common questions

What is naturopathy?

Naturopathy is a holistic approach to wellness that emphasizes lifestyle, nutrition and natural approaches. Information on this site is educational and is not a substitute for individualized medical care.

Can I use the website's information as medical advice?

No. The content is intended for general education. For diagnosis, treatment or urgent concerns, consult an appropriately qualified healthcare professional.
